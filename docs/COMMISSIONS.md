# Commissions and settlements

Each vendor has a commission rate in basis points (`commission_rate_bps`), defaulting to 1000 (10%). An administrator can change the rate with `PATCH /api/admin/vendors/{id}/commission-rate` and a JSON body such as `{"rate_bps":1250}`. Rate changes are recorded with the administrator and old/new values. New payment attempts snapshot the current rate; a later rate change cannot alter an in-flight attempt or a historical commission entry.

Commission is rounded to the nearest cent, with half cents rounded up. For each successful payment, the callback transaction stores the gross paid amount, snapshotted rate, platform commission, vendor share, currency, payment ID, and order ID in `order_commissions`. The unique order and payment constraints prevent duplicate ledger entries. A ledger entry is eligible for payout only after its payment is completed and its delivery is marked delivered.

Vendors can read their own ledger at `GET /api/vendor/commissions`. Administrators create a settlement for a vendor with `POST /api/admin/vendors/{id}/settlements`; the transaction locks eligible ledger rows and assigns them to one pending settlement, preventing concurrent or repeated settlement from claiming the same commission. The response includes the amount, currency, and commission count.

An administrator can inspect the 100 most recent records at `GET /api/admin/settlements` and finalizes a pending payout with `PATCH /api/admin/settlements/{id}` and `{"status":"paid","payout_reference":"..."}`. The payout reference is unique and serves as the external transfer audit reference. A failed transfer can be recorded with `{"status":"failed","failure_reason":"..."}`; the failed settlement remains in history while its commission rows are released for a later settlement. Pending settlements remain reserved until an administrator marks them paid or failed. Settlement creation and finalization record the responsible administrator.
